Have you heard of Heihu Spring? It sits on the east bank of Jinan's moat, with three carved stone tiger heads. Spring water bursts right out of their open mouths — the sound is so loud, it's just like a tiger roaring, and that's how it got its name. The water here is crisp, clear and naturally sweet. For generations, older Jinan residents have carried buckets here every day to fill up, then take it home to brew tea. If you come by early in the morning, you'll see the most authentic Jinan way of life: elderly locals lining up to collect water, chatting and laughing nonstop as they wait. This right here is the real, true look of that classic Jinan description: "Every household has a spring, every home has drooping willows."
