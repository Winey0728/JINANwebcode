Legend has it that the ancient Emperor Shun once farmed right here on Qianfo Mountain. Carved into the mountainside during the Sui Dynasty is the Thousand Buddha Cliff, and the Xingguo Temple sits perched firmly at the very peak.
Climb to the top and look down, and you’ll see that famous local saying come to life: half the city is mountain scenery, half is lake. The entire city of Jinan spreads out right at your feet. The story of Emperor Shun tilling the fields on Mount Li has been told and retold here for thousands of years.
