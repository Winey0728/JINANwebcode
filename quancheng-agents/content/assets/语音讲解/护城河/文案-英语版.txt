You know what makes Jinan's moat so special? It's the only moat in all of China that's fed entirely by spring water! Water from over a dozen famous springs, including Heihu Spring, Wulian Spring and Jiunv Spring, all flow into this river. The water's so clear you can see straight to the bottom, and it stays at the same comfortable temperature year round. When evening falls, hop on a painted pleasure boat, and the lights on both banks will reflect off the water. It's a truly unique experience — the only "boat tour of Spring City" you'll find anywhere in the country.
