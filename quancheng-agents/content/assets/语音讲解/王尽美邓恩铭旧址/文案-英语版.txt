This is where Wang Jinmei and Deng Enming carried out their revolutionary work in Jinan all those years ago. Both are Jinan locals, born and raised right here, and they served as delegates to the First National Congress of the Communist Party of China. They set out from this very spot to take their cause across the whole country, and made monumental contributions to the founding of the Communist Party of China.
When you walk these same paths today, it feels just like you’re following in the footsteps of these pioneers, and getting to relive those passionate, fiery revolutionary years.
