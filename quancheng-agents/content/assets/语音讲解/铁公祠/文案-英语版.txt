Tie Gong Ancestral Hall sits right on the shore of Daming Lake, built to honor Tie Xuan, a loyal official of the Ming Dynasty. Back when the Prince of Yan, Zhu Di, attacked Jinan, Tie Xuan defended the city to the very end, and simply would not let any enemy forces get inside. The people of Jinan have revered him as our guardian deity ever since. The stone tablets inside the hall record all his tales of loyalty and righteousness. Stand here on the lakeshore and look out over the vast, misty waters, and you can't help but feel a deep sense of reverence.
