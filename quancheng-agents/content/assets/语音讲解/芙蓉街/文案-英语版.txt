Furong Street is the most vibrant, down-to-earth old street in all of Jinan. Tucked away along it is Furong Spring, with time-honored local shops and trendy new snack spots lining both sides of the Qing Dynasty-era stone slab road. Come holiday season, it’s packed shoulder to shoulder, and the scent of all the good food drifts all the way down the entire street. Take a walk here, and you’ll have truly tasted the authentic flavor of Jinan’s old city.
