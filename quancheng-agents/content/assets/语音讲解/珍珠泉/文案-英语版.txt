Nestled within the grounds of Shandong Provincial People's Congress, this is the most low-key famous spring in Jinan.
Peek into the pool and you'll see strings of tiny bubbles rising from the bottom, rolling just like thousands of pearls — that's how it got the name Pearl Spring.
When the Kangxi Emperor was on his southern tour, he stayed right in this compound, and personally inscribed the three characters for "Pearl Spring".
Tucked away from all the hustle and bustle, it barely sees any tourists, and is easily the most serene hidden gem in Jinan for enjoying the city's springs.
