You know what? There’s a spring here in Jinan that’s been gurgling and bubbling up for over a thousand years. It’s Baotu Spring, known as the “Number One Spring Under Heaven”. Legend has it that when the Qianlong Emperor passed through Jinan on his southern tour, he tried tea brewed with this spring water, and immediately swapped the Jade Spring water he’d brought from Beijing for Baotu Spring water on the spot. He even took up his calligraphy brush and officially bestowed it the title “Number One Spring Under Heaven”. Look at those three jets of water, surging up from the pool floor nonstop, day and night. The highest spout can shoot up more than half a meter. Ancient people described it as “mist and clouds steam damp off Huabu Mountain, the roar of the waves shakes Daming Lake”. This spring water stays at a steady 18 degrees year-round. In winter, wispy mist curls up off the water’s surface, making the whole place feel like a fairyland. When you come to Baotu Spring, don’t forget to stop by the Wanghe Pavilion Teahouse, sit down, and brew a pot of tea with the spring water. That’s the most authentic way to experience old Jinan.
