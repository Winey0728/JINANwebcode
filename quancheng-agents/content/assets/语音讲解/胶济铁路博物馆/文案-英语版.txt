This is the Jiaozhou-Jinan Railway Museum. It was originally Jinan Station on the Jiaozhou-Jinan Railway, built by the Germans all the way back in 1904. This is the starting point of the Jiaozhou-Jinan line, and it’s borne witness to the full history of Shandong’s railways. Inside, you’ll find leftover fragments of the station’s big old clock, vintage photographs, century-old rails — every single item here tells a story of all the twists and turns of the past hundred years.
