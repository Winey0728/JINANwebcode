The Li Qingzhao Memorial Hall sits right inside Baotu Spring Park, built to honor the most remarkable female poet in all of our Chinese history. Inside you’ll find a statue of her, a biographical exhibition, and stone inscriptions of her own original calligraphy. You can feel both the bold, heroic spirit of her famous line "Better to be a hero among the living than a ghost among the dead" and the soft, plaintive lyricism of her "Seeking, seeking, searching, searching" right here.
