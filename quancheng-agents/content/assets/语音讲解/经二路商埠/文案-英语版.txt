Jing'er Road, the very heart of Jinan's modern commercial port district. Back in 1904, Jinan opened its own commercial port. On both sides of Jing'er Road, foreign trading firms, draft banks, and shops stood shoulder to shoulder, with traditional Chinese-style old buildings and Western-style foreign buildings complementing each other. This single street bore witness to the major turning point of Jinan moving from the old city into the modern era.
