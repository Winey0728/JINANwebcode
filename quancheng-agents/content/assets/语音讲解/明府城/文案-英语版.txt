Mingfu City is the old city root of our Jinan, the very core of the city’s historic heart. It was built back in the Ming Dynasty, ringed by city walls, with springs winding their way through every nook and cranny. That classic old Jinan charm of “every household has a spring, every door shaded by weeping willows” is still very much alive here. Walk down the old side streets, and you’ll find stone-paved lanes, old traditional doorways, springs flowing right past front doors — it feels just like you’ve time-traveled six hundred years back.
