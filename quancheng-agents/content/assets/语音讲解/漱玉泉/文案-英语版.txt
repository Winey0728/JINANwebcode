Shuyu Spring is right here in Baotu Spring Park. Legend says when Li Qingzhao was a kid, she used to come to this spot to wash her brushes and comb her hair. The water here is clear and shallow, held in a square stone pool, and just beside it sits the Li Qingzhao Memorial Hall.
The name "Shuyu" comes from the old phrase "shu shi zhen liu" from ancient texts—it sounds so crisp and elegant, just like Li Qingzhao's poetry. When you come here, it really feels like you've stepped straight into the youthful years of Lady Yi'an, Li Qingzhao's art name.
