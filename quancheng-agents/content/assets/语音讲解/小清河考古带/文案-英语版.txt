Spread out along both banks of the Xiaoqing River are a whole bunch of ancient sites that make up the Xiaoqinghe Archaeological Belt. From ancient prehistoric settlements all the way to Song and Yuan dynasty docks, this thousand-year-old waterway has seen it all: the transport and cultural exchange between Jinan and the Bohai Sea over the centuries. Come here, and you can feel the pulse of Jinan’s water transport history up close.
