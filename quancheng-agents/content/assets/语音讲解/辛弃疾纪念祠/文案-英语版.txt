This is the Xin Qiji Memorial Hall, dedicated to the great Southern Song Dynasty poet Xin Qiji. He was a native of our Jinan, and spent his whole life set on reclaiming the lost northern territories. You might know his iconic lines: "Drunk, I trim the lamp to gaze at my sword; in my dreams, I hear bugles call across the army encampments" — these have been passed down and cherished for thousands of years. Inside the memorial hall, you'll find a great many stone inscriptions, all preserving the patriotic devotion of this brilliant Jinan literary talent.
