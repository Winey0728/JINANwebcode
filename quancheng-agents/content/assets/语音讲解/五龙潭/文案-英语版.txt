Wulong Tan is the deepest and most tranquil of Jinan’s four major spring clusters. Legend has it that Tang Dynasty hero Qin Qiong once lived right here, and the tale of five dragons gathering in the pool has been passed down for over a thousand years. The park is home to a huge number of famous springs. Come spring, when cherry blossoms are in full bloom, the pink sea of flowers reflected in the spring pools is simply stunning. And the best part? It’s free to enter, and it’s the secret little hideaway Jinan locals love for casual strolls and spring viewing.
