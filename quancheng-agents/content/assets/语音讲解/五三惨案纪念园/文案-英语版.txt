This is the May 3rd Massacre Memorial Park, built to commemorate the brutal massacre Japanese invaders perpetrated in Jinan in 1928. The alarm bell in the park tolls ceaselessly, and a towering monument stands here, carved with the agonizing history of thousands of our fellow compatriots who perished in the tragedy. This is our Jinan's most important patriotic education base, reminding all of us to never forget history and cherish peace.
