Jiefang Pavilion sits right on the spot where the city wall was breached during the 1948 Jinan Campaign. Climb to the top and look out into the distance, and you’ll see the moat winding and curving like a ribbon, while Heihu Spring is still gushing away right there. This is where modern Jinan meets the old city’s distinct charm. Standing here, you can’t help but feel a surge of mixed emotions.
